Hi there! Welcome to my personal website!

I am an MSE student in Computer and Information Science at the University of Pennsylvania, broadly interested in understanding and improving the capabilities of **Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs)**. In a field built on discrete autoregressive transformers, I explore how **continuous generative modeling** — normalizing flows, diffusion, VAEs — can unlock richer reasoning and planning, hopefully paving the road towards AGI. ;)

Currently, I work with [Prof. Jiatao Gu](https://jiataogu.me/) on **latent chain-of-thought reasoning with normalizing flows**; our first paper, [Latent Reasoning with Normalizing Flows](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.06447), was accepted to the **COLM 2026 Workshop on Efficient Reasoning** — try the [live demo](https://nf-cot.vercel.app)! Before Penn, I earned my B.S. in Mathematics–Computer Science at UC San Diego, where I worked with [PhD. Yupeng Hou](https://yupenghou.com/) and [Prof. Julian McAuley](https://cseweb.ucsd.edu/~jmcauley/) on [BLaIR](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.03952), a benchmark for LLMs as semantic encoders in recommendation (**ACL 2026, Oral**).

Outside of research, you'll find me on a soccer field ⚽, a pickleball court 🎾, or just about any sports venues!
